At Elsy Leonso Insurance, we are a family-owned business dedicated to helping you find the best coverage to fit your needs while saving you time and money. We are committed to educating both the English- and Spanish-speaking communities on the importance of protecting themselves and their assets. Our team takes the time to thoroughly explain coverage options, ensuring you are well informed before your policy begins. With a bilingual staff specializing in sales and exceptional customer service, we are always here to answer any questions or concerns you may have.

Elsy Leonso

Agency Owner and Principal Agent

Elsy has over 20 Years of experience in the insurance industry.  Elsy specializes in Business insurance and has helped so many new entrepreneurs and small business owners understand the importance of having the correct coverage not only for their business but for their Employees as well. Elsy is the Public Notary in our office and is also MVA express certified. Elsy believes in helping the community and does so, by discounting tag and title services for the retired community and families with economic needs.

Dana Leonso

Sales Manager

Dana has over 13 years of experience in the insurance industry, where she has honed her strong customer service skills. She is passionate about educating clients, ensuring they fully understand their policies and coverage options. Recently, Dana expanded her expertise into the MVA tag and title sector, quickly excelling in this area as well.

Naomy Leonso

Tag and title Manager
