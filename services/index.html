Elsy Leonso Tag

Law in the state of Maryland requires that all residents must have their vehicles registered. As an agency authorized by the Motor Vehicle Administration, we provide license plates for new and used vehicles within minutes, with the documentation required by the state.

Before you register your vehicle at Elsy Leonso Insurance, you will need to have the proper paperwork in order. Here are a few important documents you’ll need.

Ownership Documents: You must be able to prove that the vehicle actually belongs to you. You can do this with the vehicle title from the previous owner. If you purchased the vehicle from a dealer you must have the original bill of sale. If you purchased the vehicle from a private party, and the vehicle is less than 7 years old, you must have a notarized bill of sale.
